EstrichONE is the field app for screed and flooring contractors – for site managers, crews and the office. It connects to your company's own EstrichONE server and keeps working without a network: everything is stored encrypted on the device and synced as soon as you are back online.

TODAY AT A GLANCE
• Your job with address, route, contact person and on-site notes
• Quick access to my tasks, who drives where and my week
• Notifications when the plan is released or changed

SITE FILES ALWAYS WITH YOU
• Photos and videos with captions, scan documents to PDF
• Site diary, defects, tasks and notes
• Initial inspection with checkpoints, handover with signature as PDF
• CM moisture measurements, rooms with areas and floor build-up, parties and contacts

DISPATCH ON THE GO
• Who drives where: crews, vehicles and sites in driving order
• Quick dispatch: assign a site, move it, put it on the next free day
• Swap workers, vehicles or whole crews – with conflict check
• Release the plan and see every change since the release

COMMUNICATION
• Chat between office and site – one-to-one and crew chats
• Helpdesk: report a problem with a photo, follow the answers
• Push notifications for assignments, plan changes and releases
• Customer directory with call, message, email and route

RELIABLE AND SECURE
• Your data stays on your company's server – not with us
• Protection against data loss: read-only mode when the server has been unreachable for a while
• Notices during maintenance and when backups fail
• Encrypted storage on the device, unlock with Face ID or PIN
• Personal devices and shared devices with user switching
• No tracking, no ads

Try it without a server: tap “Demo ausprobieren” on the start screen.
Production use requires an EstrichONE server (free, self-hosted). The app interface is in German.
